Swing Mirror (Auto)

Getting started

1Click Start camera and pick your iPhone. Stand so your whole body is in frame.
2Click Load video and choose a pro clip filmed from the same angle as you (down-the-line to down-the-line, or face-on to face-on).
3Set fps to match the clip, then click Analyse pro and wait for the scan.
4Turn on Auto sync. Move through your swing and the pro follows.
